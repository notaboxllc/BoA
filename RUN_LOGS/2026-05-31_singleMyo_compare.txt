## Key observations:
##
## 1. Internal joint angles MATCH well: theta_RL 5° gap (much smaller than
##    head_r gap of half a micron), theta_LM agrees to 0.14°. The myosin's
##    INTERNAL shape is statistically similar on both paths.
##
## 2. Head position is COMPLETELY DIFFERENT: GPU head wanders to a mean
##    radial distance of 0.53 µm with max 1.05 µm. Rod length is 0.080 µm
##    + lever 0.008 + motor 0.020 = 0.108 µm. The GPU head_r at 1.05 µm
##    is 10× the assembly's total contour length — the whole myosin has
##    drifted from its anchor.
##
## 3. CPU head stays close to the anchor: head_r mean 0.061, max 0.122 µm
##    (consistent with thermal tumbling within one body length). head_z
##    mean = -0.050, exactly the anchor z-value — the rod stays vertical-ish.
##
## 4. Absolute rod tilt is nearly isotropic on both paths (89° vs 87°
##    mean, both with ~0.71 SD), so the assembly orientation distribution
##    is similar in shape — the difference is that on GPU the assembly
##    translates freely from its anchor.

## ROOT CAUSE — identified during the diagnostic:
##
## MyosinFixed.jointConstraints() = super.jointConstraints() + applyRodFixedPtForce().
## The second call is the rod-tail anchor spring (rod end1 pulled toward myFixedPt).
##
## On the GPU path, Myosin.MyosinThreads.divideAndConquer short-circuits the
## per-Myosin CPU dispatch when `useGPU && !DIAG_CPU_JOINTS` (Myosin.java:77-88),
## setting all jobDiv[i]=0. So MyosinFixed.jointConstraints() is NEVER called.
## The GPU jointsKernel (GPUMoveThing.java:286+) only computes the 4 inter-segment
## forces/torques (lever-motor force/torque, rod-lever force/torque) — it has
## ZERO references to MyosinFixed, myFixedPt, or applyRodFixedPtForce. The
## anchoring spring is silently dropped on the GPU path.
##
## Implication: in the production gliding-assay GPU run, 14000 MyosinFixed
## myosins are NOT anchored. The CPU baseline runs through the per-Myosin
## dispatch and DOES apply the rod-tail anchor. This explains:
##   - the 55 nm head_z gap (CPU rod stays vertical, GPU rod drifts)
##   - the -42% bindEvents drop (GPU motors drift away from filaments)
##   - the ~24% gliding-velocity drop (fewer effective binding sites)
##   - why the DIAG_CPU_JOINTS path passes (it routes through MyosinFixed
##     and runs applyRodFixedPtForce as part of the CPU dispatch)
##   - why Part B (CPU-side delta-add of GPU joint output) also failed
##     (the per-Myosin CPU dispatch was also short-circuited there, so
##     applyRodFixedPtForce was missing on Part B too)
##
## This is the minimal reproduction the prior seven joint diagnostics couldn't
## see, because they all ran in the full gliding-assay topology where binding
## physics partially compensates for the missing anchor.
