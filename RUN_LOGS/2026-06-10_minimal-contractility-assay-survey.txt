SURVEY — Minimal contractility assay (myosin-generated tension between two anchored anti-parallel filaments)
Date: 2026-06-10   Mode: read-only survey, no source changes.

Target assay recap: two anti-parallel, overlapping, laterally-offset stiff filaments in a long
narrow box; one minifilament in the overlap (turnover off); both filament PLUS (barbed) ends
anchored at the outer box ends; myosin walks plus-ward → inward (contractile) tension at the
anchors; MEASURE that tension. Crux = (a) anchor a plus-end, (b) read tension off it.

ITEM 2 — FILAMENT END ANCHORING  *** CRUX (a) ***
================================================================================
THREE candidate mechanisms exist; ranked by fitness for a hard plus-end anchor:

(A) BENCHMARK PIN — post-integration endpoint correction  [BEST FIT, REUSE THIS]
    BoxOfActin.applyBenchmarkPins() — BoxOfActin.java:1853-1865, called each step at line ~1242
    immediately after moveThing(), gated by Env.benchmarkFilament.
    Mechanism: after the segment integrates freely, translate the terminal segment so its pinned
    endpoint snaps back exactly to the fixed anchor:
        firstSeg.incCoord(anchor1 - firstSeg.end1); firstSeg.initialize();
        lastSeg .incCoord(anchor2 - lastSeg.end2 ); lastSeg .initialize();
    - This is a HARD positional constraint on ONE endpoint of ONE terminal segment. Exactly the
      boundary condition the assay needs.
    - Per-segment selectable in principle: it operates on whichever FilSegment + whichever end
      (end1 or end2) you point it at. Currently hardwired to the single deflFil chain's two ends.
    - To reuse: generalize from one chain to a small registry of (segment, whichEnd, anchorPt)
      pin records, and call the same incCoord/initialize correction for each, every step.
    NOTE: it pins the TERMINAL SEGMENT'S ENDPOINT, not a whole rigid filament — interior segments
    still flex. That is fine (and desired: tension propagates down the chain to the pinned end).

(B) AnchorNode — AnchorNode.java (extends ProteinNode)
    Constructor sets bTransGam = (1e6,1e6,1e6) (~immobile drag) and overrides step()/moveThing()
    to no-ops → a node frozen in space. BUT a filament does not attach to it directly; you'd need a
    tether link (StickyNode/NodeLink/FilLink tether spring) between the node and the filament end.
    That is a SOFT anchor (spring), more moving parts, and the tether mechanics (NodeLink) are
    no-op in gliding production. Lower fit than (A) unless we deliberately want a compliant anchor.

(C) "static actin" param idiom (noMonomersSimd:true + all turnover rates off)
    Used by ParameterFiles/boa10-miniFil-staticActin and boa10-singleMiniFil. IMPORTANT: "static"
    here means NO MONOMER DYNAMICS (rigid rods, no poly/depoly/aging) — the filaments STILL
    translate and rotate freely under force. This is NOT spatial anchoring. Useful for the assay's
    "no turnover / rigid rod" requirement, but does NOT pin the plus-ends. Combine with (A).

ProteinNode.fixedNode (ProteinNode.java:24) — skips moveThing() when true; a node-freeze flag, not
a filament-end mechanism. There is NO isFixed/pinned/frozen flag on FilSegment itself.

ITEM 3 — TENSION / END-FORCE READOUT  *** CRUX (b) ***
================================================================================
No turnkey "reaction force at anchor" readout exists. But the cleanest quantity is already
computed every step:

- Net force on a segment: Thing.soaForceSum[] via getForceSumX/Y/Z() (Thing.java:549-551),
  accumulated through incForceSum() (Thing.java:470-489) from all force phases (Brownian, chain,
  joints, MOTOR cross-bridge force, boundary). For the PINNED terminal segment, the net force it
  accumulates in the step BEFORE applyBenchmarkPins() snaps it back IS the reaction force the
  anchor must supply — i.e. the transmitted tension. Project onto the filament long axis
  (Pt3D.Dot(uVecAsPt3D(), F)) to get the axial (contractile) component.
      => CLEANEST READOUT: tension ≈ dot( firstSeg.uVec , (Fx,Fy,Fz) ) read from getForceSum*()
         on the anchored terminal segment, sampled each step just before the pin correction.

- Axial-force accumulators ALREADY on FilSegment: end1AxialF / end2AxialF (FilSegment.java:230-231),
  incEnd1AxialForce/incEnd2AxialForce (~2959-2965), zeroed each step (~1259-1260). Today they are
  fed ONLY by crosslink forces (e.g. line ~1554 incEnd2AxialForce(Dot(uVec,F))), not by the full
  force sum. They are the natural field to repurpose: have the anchored end record its net axial
  load here for a clean per-end tension trace.

- Equivalent alternative: tension = (pin correction displacement)·(drag/deltaT). The incCoord
  applied in applyBenchmarkPins is exactly the un-restored free motion; multiplying by the
  effective drag over dt recovers the same force. Reading getForceSum* directly is simpler.

MISSING: wiring either of the above into an output column / [STATS] line / WebSocket topic. Trivial
to add (mirror the benchmark's buildBenchmarkJson pattern, BoxOfActin.java:1905+).

ITEM 4 — PLACED-FILAMENT IC (position, orientation, polarity)
================================================================================
REUSABLE:
- General placed-filament builder = the FilSegment constructor itself:
    new FilSegment(Pt3D initCoord, Pt3D initUVec, int filID, int monomerCt, boolean fromFile)
    FilSegment.java:283. initCoord = center position, initUVec = long-axis orientation (unit),
    filID = -1 for auto-assign, monomerCt = length in monomers. This is the generalizable primitive.
- makeGlidingAssayFilament() — FilSegment.java:3457 — is just a thin wrapper around that constructor
  with HARD-CODED placement: loc = (boxX/2 - filLength/2 - 1.5*segLen, 0, 0), uVec = (1,0,0). Good
  template, but not parameterized for two offset anti-parallel filaments — call the constructor
  directly instead.
- Polarity: encoded by monomer chain direction, minusMon→plusMon (FilSegment.java:251). Chain built
  minus-end first, monomers added toward PLUSEND (makeInitialMonomers, ~471-478). Geometric end1/end2
  are coord ∓/± (length/2)·uVec. *** CONFIRM AT BUILD TIME which geometric end (end1 vs end2) is the
  plus/barbed end for a given uVec sign, and which way the motor walks *** — established empirically
  by the gliding-assay glide direction; not spelled out in a single constant. Anti-parallel = two
  filaments with opposite uVec (e.g. +X and −X), so their plus ends point to opposite box ends.

  MyoMiniFilament.java:75. (The :64 single-arg ctor randomizes orientation; the random-population

  (MyoMiniFilament.biochemStep ~299-302; Env.myoMiniLifetime, Env.java:487, default 10 s). Raise
  myoMiniLifetime to a huge value (e.g. 1e9) to disable turnover. (CONFIRMED — matches memo.)

ITEM 6 — NARROW BOX + LATERAL CONFINEMENT
================================================================================
REUSABLE:
- Chamber.makeABox() — Chamber.java:102 — builds a box CENTERED at origin from Env.boxXDim/boxYDim/
  boxZDim (Env.java:368-380). Walls at ±dim/2. A long narrow box = boxXDim large, boxYDim/boxZDim
  small (the gliding configs already do 14×2×0.5; go narrower e.g. boxYDim:0.3, boxZDim:0.2).
  Reached by default in makeCrucible() (BoxOfActin.java:2644) when not a bug-shaped crucible.
- Lateral confinement of the minifilament: comes "for free" from a thin box (the box-boundary
  kernel F1 keeps things inside). No separate per-object lateral clamp exists; a thin slab in
  Y and/or Z confines the minifilament to the filament region. If tighter coupling is needed,
  that's a build-time addition, not an existing feature.

ITEM 7 — STIFFNESS REGIME (phalloidin)
================================================================================
CONFIRMED from JOURNAL (lines 1729, 10779, 12731) and param files:
- Phalloidin-stiff regime: fracMove ≈ 0.0573, fracR = 1.0, fracMoveTorq = 0.01.
  (CLAUDE.md/memo's "0.05725 / 1.0 / 0.1" — the 0.1 is an older fracMoveTorq; the validated gliding
  baseline used 0.01. Pin the exact value during calibration.)
- Params: Env.java:127-139 fracMove/fracR/fracMoveTorq, ALL .setMutableAtRuntime() (live-tunable).
  Applied globally per run (PAIRS chain bending). Set them in the parameter file.
- Intuition (from comments): smaller fracR → softer restoring force; larger fracMoveTorq → stiffer
  torsion. For a non-buckling stiff rod under compressive contractile load, keep fracR high (=1.0).
- Caveat: the deflection benchmark's own calibrated trio (fracMove=0.5/fracR=0.1/fracMoveTorq=0.265,
  CLAUDE.md) is a DIFFERENT target (ratio≈1 at aeta=0.1). Use the phalloidin trio for the assay.

REUSE AS-IS:
  - FilSegment(initCoord, initUVec, -1, monCt, false)  → place each of the 2 filaments explicitly.
  - new MyoMiniFilament(overlapCenter, overlapUVec)     → one minifilament in the overlap.

    (from boa10-miniFil-staticActin) → rigid non-turning-over filaments.

ADD (new, small):

     begin()/makeCrucible): instantiate fil A (uVec +X, plus end at +X box wall), fil B (uVec −X,

     axialTension = Dot(uVec, getForceSum*) (optionally into end{1,2}AxialF), and emit it on a

  4. A gating flag (e.g. -contractility or an Env param) to select this IC + the pin/readout path,
