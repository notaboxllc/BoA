CONCLUSIONS
-----------
1. JULICHER/TriMem/FreeDTS form S2 -> 8*pi cleanly (h^2 convergence, ~4x error reduction per subdivision;
   0.05% at nu=4, 0.003% at nu=6). kappa multiplies the energy DIRECTLY: E_bend = kappa * S2. No sqrt(3)/2,
   no geometry-dependent fudge. This is the form to implement. Matches FreeDTS energy AND TriMem gradient.

2. CRUDE Seung-Nelson Sum(1-n.n) is anisotropic (sphere != cylinder): S1 sits at ~0.299*8pi on the sphere,
   nowhere near a clean 8*pi*kappa. (Literature is itself split on the crude-form sphere constant -- the
   "general/cylinder" mapping kappa=(sqrt3/2)kd predicts 8pi*sqrt3/2=21.77, while the sphere-specific
   mapping kappa=kd/sqrt3 predicts 8pi/sqrt3=14.51; our once-per-edge icosphere measures ~7.52. The point
   is precisely that there is NO single clean constant -- which is why we reject the crude form.) REJECTED.

KEY LITERATURE (full citations in JOURNAL / design doc)
- FreeDTS (Pezeshkian 2024): shape-operator per-vertex curvature; A_v = (1/3) sum incident areas (barycentric);
  MC-only, energy-only (no forces). GPL-2.0.
- TriMem (Siggel 2022): SAME edge/dihedral (Itzykson/Julicher) curvature H_i=(1/4A_i)sum l_e theta_e, A_i
  barycentric; E = kappa sum_i 2 c_i^2/A_i; provides the ANALYTIC GRADIENT (3 primitive derivatives:
  d l_e/dr, d A_face/dr = 0.5 n x e_opp, d theta_e/dr hinge-angle gradient). GPL-3.0. NOT the Meyer cotangent
  operator (that's Mem3DG).
- Seung-Nelson 1988 / Schmidt-Fraternali 2012 / Fedosov 2010 / arXiv:2503.00302: crude-dihedral coefficient
  is geometry-dependent (the reason we don't use it).
