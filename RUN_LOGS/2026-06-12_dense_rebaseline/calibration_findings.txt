MECHANISM: with maxLinksOnSeg=10, crosslinks pile up between already-adjacent filament
PAIRS (bundling -> components of size ~2), rather than bridging new filaments. More links
and longer reach BUNDLE harder; they do not connect the box. At 10x areal density the
mean filament spacing (~0.6um) leaves too few distinct neighbours per filament.

--- Box-volume / F-actin units note ---
Chamber.makeABox sets boxVolume = 8*dimX*dimY*dimZ (= 8*10*10*0.5 = 400 um^3) while
filaments are seeded in [-0.5*boxXDim, +0.5*boxXDim]^... = the 10x10x0.5 = 50 um^3 region.
microMolarChangePerMonomer (and hence actinConc and the reported fActinUM) is normalised
by the 400 um^3 reference, so the simulation-internal F-actin is 8x lower than the
geometric concentration in the occupied 50 um^3. Geometric F-actin at 10x density is
~13.7 uM (on the prompt's 12-20 uM target); the sim-internal value is ~1.7 uM. The biochem
rates (poly/depoly draw from actinConc) operate in the sim-internal units, so this is an
internal-consistency convention, not a correctness bug — but it must be reconciled before
"F-actin uM" is quoted as a target.
